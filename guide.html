What is RTH?
Overview of the platform

The Risk Training Academy (RTH) is an internal learning and reference tool built for Risk & Compliance Operations investigators. It consolidates procedure documentation, interactive decision tools, and training simulations into a single place — accessible on company VPN from any device.

📚 Knowledge Base

Searchable procedure docs, full glossary/dictionary, and reference materials. In progress — full database coming soon.

⚖️ Decision Matrix

Interactive decision trees for every case type. Answer questions, get the correct action and reason code instantly.

🎯 Training Simulator

Work real compliance scenarios across 3 difficulty levels. 40 scenarios covering all clients, events, and edge cases.

🌍 Where in the World?

Geography and language identification training — scripts, country names, name origins, and sanctioned territories. 135 questions across 4 modes.

Knowledge Base
How to search, browse, and use the KB
1
Search for anything
Type any term, procedure name, reason code, client name, or keyword into the search bar. Results update live as you type. Try: batch_type_a_registrant, sanctioned countries, 2/3 name match, Cleared by DOB.
2
Use the sidebar to filter by category
Dictionary — individual term definitions only. Originals — condensed summaries of source procedure docs. Tech Edits — clean, restructured rewrites of the same docs. Use the client tabs (Program Alpha, Program Beta, Program Gamma, Pending) to filter to a specific client.
3
Click a card to expand it
The preview text disappears and the full content appears. Click again to collapse. The card header stays visible so you always know what you're reading.
4
Copy source doc URLs
Every relevant card has a 📋 Copy source URL button. Click it, then paste the copied URL into a fresh browser tab to view the official full document on the compliance investigations site. Direct navigation is blocked by the site's security headers — this is the workaround.
5
Understand the badges
Glossary — dictionary definition. [Client] · Original — condensed summary of the source document. [Client] · Tech Edit — restructured rewrite. Original and Tech Edit cover the same content, different format. Use whichever works for you.
⚙️ In Progress: The Knowledge Base database is still being built. Originals and Tech Edits are condensed — always reference the official source docs for audit and escalation decisions. The Dictionary, Decision Matrix, Training Simulator, and Geography Training are complete.

Decision Matrix
Getting the right action and reason code in seconds
1
Select your matrix type
Four options: With PII (individuals with PII submitted), Program Gamma — No PII (Program Gamma cases before PII), Companies (Program Alpha/Program Beta company cases), Batch Free Dev (Program Alpha batch_type_a_registrant event).
2
Answer each question about your case
The matrix walks you through: entity type → name match → DOB → location. Answer honestly about your actual case data. The progress bar at the top shows where you are in the decision tree.
3
Read the result
The result card shows your action (CLEAR, ESCALATE, or SUSPEND), the exact reason code to use, and an explanation. Your full decision path is shown below.
4
Click "↩ New Case" to start over
Resets the current matrix type. Change the matrix type using the tabs at the top.
Tip: When in doubt after reviewing the matrix — escalate. The Decision Matrix is a decision support tool, not a replacement for your judgment on individual case details.

Training Simulator
40 scenarios, 3 difficulty levels
1
Choose your difficulty
🌿 Initiate — 13 scenarios covering clear-cut decisions: false positives, entity type mismatches, obvious variances. ⚡ Investigator — 13 mixed scenarios: China naming convention, YOB shortcuts, 2/3 name match, expired PII, edge cases. 🔱 Olympian — 14 hard scenarios: full address match exceptions, ownership traps, previously suspended cases, sanctioned country connections in System Notes.
2
Work each case
Review the Screened Entity and Matched Data just like in CMS. Choose the correct action from 4 options. You have 3 lives (🌿🌿🌿). Lose all three and the session ends early.
3
Read the feedback
Every question — right or wrong — shows a full explanation of the correct reasoning. Wrong answers are often the most educational. Streak of 3+ correct answers earns a bonus.
4
Check your score and rank
Scores determine your rank: Trainee → Investigator I → Investigator II → Senior Investigator → Lead Investigator. Scenarios are shuffled randomly each session so repeat plays feel fresh.

Where in the World?
Geography and language training — 135 questions, 4 modes

🔤 Script Intel

Identify writing systems from text samples. Arabic, Cyrillic, Hangul, Devanagari, Georgian, Bengali and more — including compliance-critical examples like 조선 (North Korea) and ایران (Iran).

🗺️ Country Decoder

Identify countries from their native-language names. Deutschland, 中国, 조선민주주의인민공화국 (North Korea 🚨), جمهوری اسلامی ایران (Iran 🚨), and 35 more.

👤 Name Origins

Identify the likely geographic origin of names. Zhang Wei 张伟, Wang Fang 王芳, José, Giuseppe, Nguyễn, Kim, Fatima, Reza (Iranian 🚨), and more.

🚨 Sanctioned or Safe?

The most compliance-critical mode — 39 questions covering all 3 sanctioned countries, all 5 Ukrainian sanctioned regions, and real scenario-based address questions (Mariupol, Sevastopol, Kharkiv, etc.).

🚨 Compliance-Critical questions are highlighted with red badges and worth 150 points (vs 100 for regular questions). Getting these wrong shows a detailed explanation — these are the questions that matter most on the job.

Frequently Asked Questions
Click any question to expand
Why do the source doc links say "Copy URL" instead of opening directly? +
The compliance investigations site (compliance.vilecorp.com) sets a Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy security header that prevents pages from being opened via direct links from external domains. This is a server-side security setting we can't override from RTH. The workaround: click 📋 Copy source URL, then open a fresh browser tab and paste the URL. This bypasses the security restriction entirely.
Are the Knowledge Base articles the official procedure documents? +
No — always use the official source docs for audit and escalation decisions. The KB currently contains: Originals (condensed summaries of source documents, not verbatim) and Tech Edits (restructured rewrites for clarity). Neither is a verbatim copy of the official procedures. The official docs live at compliance.vilecorp.com. Full database migration with complete doc content is in progress.
Do I need VPN to use RTH? +
Yes. RTH is hosted on GitHub Pages (pages.github.com), which is only accessible from Apple's internal network. You need to be on company VPN or on-site to access it. If you see a connection error, check your VPN status first.
Why is the Knowledge Base "in progress"? +
We're building a proper database backend to store the full procedure documents with real-time update capability — so content can be updated without touching code. Until then, the KB has condensed summaries with links to the official source docs. The Dictionary, Decision Matrix, Training Simulator, and Geography Training are fully complete and ready to use now.
How do I send a colleague directly to the Decision Matrix (or Training, or any section)? +
Use these direct links — they work on any device on VPN:

Knowledge Base: #kb
Decision Matrix: #matrix
Training Simulator: #training
Geography Game: geography-game.html

You can also navigate to any section and copy the URL from your browser's address bar — it updates live as you switch tabs.
What's the difference between "Original" and "Tech Edit" in the Knowledge Base? +
Originals are condensed summaries of the source procedure documents — same structure and content as the official docs, but shorter. They include a "Copy source URL" button to access the full official version.

Tech Edits are complete rewrites of the same content by a technical writer — cleaner structure, less redundancy, decision tables instead of paragraph descriptions. Same coverage, better readability.

Both cover the same material. Use whichever format works better for you.
Is the Training Simulator based on real compliance cases? +
The Training Simulator scenarios are based directly on patterns described in the official procedure documents — the same situations and decision logic you encounter in CMS. They use anonymized, illustrative names and details (e.g., "Johnny Appleseed," "GG Technologies Inc") from the procedure docs themselves. No actual case data is used.
Does my Training Simulator score save? +
Not currently — scores reset each session. This is one of the planned database features: persistent score tracking, leaderboards, and progress over time. For now, use it as a practice tool and note your accuracy percentage at the end of each session.
Can I use RTH on mobile? +
Yes — RTH is responsive and works on iPhone and iPad (with VPN). The Knowledge Base sidebar collapses on small screens. The Training Simulator and Decision Matrix work fully on mobile. The Geography Game is fully mobile-friendly. The best experience is on desktop due to the amount of content.
What does "RTH" stand for, and what happened to "CMS"? +
RTH = Risk Training Academy. The 🎓 caduceus symbol in the logo is a nod to CMS (the Greek messenger god who carries the caduceus) — since CMS (the tool investigators use every day) stands for Case Management System. RTH was renamed from CMS to avoid confusion with the CMS tool itself.
The Where in the World? game doesn't change when I switch location themes. Is that a bug? +
That's intentional. The Where in the World? game is a standalone page with its own design. Location themes only apply to the main RTH site (Knowledge Base, Decision Matrix, Training Simulator). This was a deliberate design decision to keep the game's visual design consistent.
How current is the procedure content? +
The KB content was built from source documents as of May 2026. The official source docs at compliance.vilecorp.com are the authoritative version — always check there for the latest updates, especially after major policy changes (like Syria's removal from the sanctions list in November 2025). The RTH dictionary notes update dates for key terms.
I found an error or want to suggest new content — who do I contact? +
Reach out to Krystine Hall via Slack or the compliance investigations team channel. New training scenarios, dictionary terms, and content corrections are actively welcome. Bug reports for the RTH site itself are also very helpful — describe what you were doing and what happened.
