# KB_02 — Glossary
### Compliance Terms & Definitions
Last Updated: May 2026

---

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **AML** | Anti-Money Laundering. Legal controls requiring institutions and entities to prevent, detect, and report money laundering activities. |
| **Program Alpha (Apple Media Products)** | Manages iTunes, the Apple App Store, and Apple Services. Compliance screens anyone with access to iTunes Connect accounts — the platform used to distribute media. Vendors, banks, and bank account holders are also screened in this client. |
| **API (Apple Payments Inc.)** | Apple Payments Inc. — a subsidiary of Apple Inc. supporting the disbursement platform for Apple Card daily cash rebates and direct Apple Cash-to-Apple Card bill payments. |
| **Apple Developer Program** | A program giving customers worldwide access to beta software, app capabilities, testing tools, and analytics. |
| **CIP** | Customer Identification Program. A U.S. requirement for financial institutions to verify a customer's identity. |
| **Client** | The application or program from which the screened case data originated. |
| **Close Name Match** | When a company's brand name matches the Matched Data but the legal identifier differs (e.g., "Biotech" vs. "Biotech LLC"). See Match Determination Guidelines for full criteria. |
| **Compliance** | Being in accordance with established guidelines or specifications, or the process of achieving that state. |
| **Common Name** | A name shared by many individuals (e.g., "John Smith") or a generic name used by many companies. |
| **Core Case** | The first Transaction ID in a sequence of cases. Contains the original screened name (including any DBA names) and the original Matched Data hits. |
| **DBA (Doing Business As)** | A trade name, assumed name, or fictitious business name — a registered operating name that differs from the company's official legal name. |
| **Delta List Update** | Changes made to a sanctions list when entities are added or removed. |
| **Developer Agreement** | An agreement granting customers access to developer conferences, technical talks, tools, events, and services. |
| **Disbursement Agreement** | An agreement between Apple and a customer for receiving Apple Card daily cash. |
| **DOW Jones** | Provider of sanctions screening data and ownership/control research used to help businesses comply with sanctions regulations. |
| **DRI** | Directly Responsible Individual. |
| **Due Diligence** | Taking every precaution and conducting thorough investigation to identify businesses or individuals subject to sanctions. |
| **DUNS Number** | A unique nine-digit number that identifies a business. Issued by Dun & Bradstreet. |
| **Enhanced Due Diligence** | Additional investigative measures applied due to elevated risk. |
| **Entity** | An individual, organization, business, or company that has the legal capacity to enter a contract or agreement. |
| **Entity Type** | Whether the screened party is an individual, company, bank, vessel, etc. |
| **Event** | The interaction, transaction, enrollment, or account update that triggered CMS to open a case. |
| **False Hit / False Positive** | A system-identified potential match caused by a common name, missing information, or a partial match — not a true sanctions match. |
| **Full Match** | The entire name of the Screened Entity matches the name on the sanctions list. |
| **CMS (Case Management System)** | The case management tool used to review and action compliance cases. |
| **IDMS (Identity Management Services)** | Provides authentication and authorization services and is the repository for all Apple identities. The Program Gamma compliance client screens Apple IDs when customers add payment methods to Apple Wallet. |
| **Inclusion Term** | A trigger created when a Screened Entity name contains a word on Apple's corporate compliance inclusion list. Always a direct escalation. |
| **IP Address** | A numerical code identifying the geographic location of a computer accessing the internet. |
| **KYC** | Know Your Customer. A business process of verifying customer identity. |
| **LexID** | A unique identifier assigned to individuals by LexisNexis. |
| **Matched Data Hit** | A specific entry on a sanctions list that the system has identified as a potential match to the Screened Entity. A single case may have multiple Matched Data Hits. |
| **OFAC** | The Office of Foreign Assets Control. A U.S. Department of the Treasury agency that administers and enforces economic and trade sanctions based on U.S. foreign policy and national security goals. |
| **OFAC List / SDN List** | The Specially Designated Nationals and Blocked Persons List. A list of individuals, groups, and entities (terrorists, narcotics traffickers, etc.) that U.S. persons are generally prohibited from dealing with. |
| **Ownership** | The structure of who ultimately controls and benefits from a company, whether directly or indirectly. |
| **Partial Match** | Only a portion of information matches — not the entire name. |
| **PCD (Pending Cancel Date)** | The date on which a suspended case will automatically cancel if no action is taken. |
| **PII (Personally Identifiable Information)** | Information that can be used to identify a specific individual, such as a passport number, national ID, or date of birth. |
| **Risk Category** | A rating (Low, Medium, or High) based on the Risk Score. |
| **Risk Score** | A numerical score calculated by CMS based on the similarity of the Screened Entity to the Matched Data hit. Higher similarity = higher risk score. |
| **Reverse** | To search for related information starting from an initial piece of evidence (e.g., using an email to find a name). |
| **Sanctions List** | A list of sanctioned entities, individuals, and banks maintained by regulatory bodies such as OFAC (U.S.), the EU (CFSP), and UK HM Treasury. |
| **SAR** | Suspicious Activity Report. A document filed with the Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) following a suspected incident. |
| **Screened Entity** | The individual, company, or entity that triggered a potential match on a sanctions list and is under review. |
| **SLA (Service Level Agreement)** | The target timeframe within which a case must be reviewed and resolved. All current clients: 48 hours. |
| **TID (Transaction ID)** | The unique ID number CMS assigns to each case. |
| **True Match** | Research confirms the Screened Entity is the same person or entity as the sanctions list hit. |
| **Weak Alias / Low Quality AKA** | A generic or common nickname that may be used in place of a legal name, and that alone is not sufficient to establish a true match. See OFAC guidance: https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/topic/1646 |
| **Program Beta (Worldwide Developer Relations)** | The Apple team supporting Apple developers. The Program Beta compliance client screens developer enrollments. |
| **2/3 Name Match** | A criteria specific to Program Gamma cases (without PII): a screened entity's full name matches 2 of 3 names in the Matched Data's name. See Match Determination Guidelines for full criteria. |
